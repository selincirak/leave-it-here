Leave It Here

Privacy

effective 18 oct 2026

The short version: nothing you drop here ever leaves your phone. There's no account, no server, and no one but you has access to what you save.

What stays on your device

Everything you leave — text, links, photos, the times you ask to be reminded — is written to a local database inside the app's own storage on your phone. None of it is uploaded, synced, backed up, or sent anywhere. If you uninstall Leave It Here, all of it is deleted with it.

text & links
stored in a local database on your device
photos
saved as files in the app's private storage
reminder times
scheduled as local notifications on your phone, nowhere else

What we collect

nothing

Leave It Here has no servers to send data to. There's no account to create, no analytics tracking what you do in the app, no advertising, and nothing shared with third parties — because there's no mechanism in the app that could share it. We, the people who made this app, cannot see your notes, your photos, or what you've saved. There's simply no pipe from your phone to us.

Permissions the app asks for

Each one is used only for the feature it's named after, only when you use that feature:

photo library
to let you attach a photo when you drop something — the app never browses your library on its own
notifications
to show the reminders you set — local only, not push notifications from a server

Sensitive content

Leave It Here can recognize when something you've typed looks like a password, PIN, or similar — a plain pattern match that happens entirely on your device. It never reads the content aloud to a server to make that call. You decide whether to keep it hidden (masked until you tap to reveal) or visible, and that choice, like everything else, stays local to your phone.

Children

Leave It Here isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone — which, as above, is true for every user regardless of age, since nothing is collected from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes — say, a future version adds optional sync — this page will be updated first, with the effective date above moved to match, before that feature ships.
